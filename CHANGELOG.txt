v8.6.136 — GAME 2 GOAL REPAIR + MONEY MODE HISTORY
============================================================
EDIT STATS / GOAL REPAIR
- Restored the Goals Against +/- control while Edit Stats is active.
- The GA control remains hidden during normal saved-game review.
- Raising/lowering GA in Edit Stats no longer creates synthetic score-correction events.
- Historical goal events remain intact so an older game can temporarily reveal previously recorded goals.
- Individual goal delete buttons remain the permanent way to remove the exact incorrect goal without disturbing later goal timestamps or periods.

HISTORICAL MONEY MODE STATS
- Added compatibility for games recorded before the general High-Danger Saves field existed.
- Stats now use highDangerSaves when present, otherwise fall back to the older moneyHighDangerSaves value.

- Newer games that contain both fields are not double-counted.

============================================================

- Keeps v8.6.134 exact-goal deletion and recorded goal-time behavior intact.

============================================================
v8.6.134 — DELETE EXACT GOALS + GOAL TIMES
============================================================
GAME RECAP / EDITING
- Each recorded Goal Against now shows the actual clock time it was recorded, alongside its period.
- In Edit Stats mode, each individual goal has its own delete button.
- Deleting a goal removes that exact event instead of subtracting the newest goals from the final total.
- Goals recorded later keep their original timestamps, periods, and goal-detail tags.
- Goals Against, shots faced, save percentage, period totals, recap values, and saved stats recalculate from the corrected game.
- Breakaway Goal deletion also removes that goal's associated breakaway count.
- Removed the saved-game Goals Against +/- editor so goal corrections are event-specific rather than chronology-destroying total adjustments.
- Opponent score is displayed from the corrected Goal Against events instead of being independently edited.

v8.6.133 — GAME 2 LIVE SAFETY + OPPONENT GOALIE STATS
============================================================
LIVE TRACKING
- Added screen Wake Lock while actively tracking a game on supported browsers/devices.
- The app requests the wake lock again after returning to The Crease if the browser released it.
- Goal Against now requires an intentional 0.8-second press-and-hold instead of a normal tap.
- Added a visible HOLD TO RECORD cue and hold-progress indicator to Goal Against.

SCORE CORRECTIONS
- Added an explicit Opponent score field to the final game recap.
- Correcting the opponent score now updates the game's goalie Goals Against total used by recap cards and season statistics.
- Score corrections are also recorded as correction events so saved period/event data do not silently keep the old aggregate total.
- Existing inline Goals Against edits now record the same correction delta.

OPPONENT GOALIE TRACKING
- Opponent-goalie stats are calculated automatically from Our Shots and Our Goals.
- Added Opponent Goalie to the full game recap: shots faced, saves, GA, and SV%.
- Added THEIR SV% as a selectable metric in Stats → Splits → By Opponent.
- Opponent Recap now includes season-to-date opposing-goalie SV%, saves, and GA for that opponent.
- Every individual opponent-game snapshot now includes that game's opposing-goalie shots, saves, GA, and SV%.
- No additional live-game tapping is required.
