v8.6.157 — Manage Opponents
- Added Manage Opponents directly to New Game setup.
- Opponents can be removed from future New Game choices even when historical games used that opponent.
- Removing an opponent choice does not delete, rename, or modify any saved game history or statistics.
- Removed opponent names stay hidden even when they exist in past games; explicitly using the name again restores it to future choices.
- Preserves v8.6.156 Add Past Game and v8.6.155 live-session identity safety unchanged.

v8.6.156 — Add Past Game
- Added a safe Add Past Game workflow for restoring completed historical games from known stats.
- Historical entries never become live/resumable sessions and cannot overwrite an existing saved game.
- Supports date, opponent, arena, game type, score, period-by-period shots/saves/GA, breakaways, high-danger saves, optional goal details, Money Mode, Protected Game, Player of the Game, and notes.
- Validates score/GA, breakaway, high-danger, and Money Mode totals before saving.
- Preserves v8.6.155 live-session identity safety and scoring behavior unchanged.
